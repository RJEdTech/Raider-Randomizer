# Raider Randomizer

A complete classroom toolkit for Regis Jesuit High School teachers — pick a student with names or without them, make groups, build a seating chart, and run a timer, all in one tool.

**[Launch Raider Randomizer →](https://rjedtech.github.io/Raider-Randomizer/)**

## Three modes

Pick one at the top of the screen. Each one fills the page, with its own **⚙ Settings** panel that collapses out of the way so the only thing projected is the thing students should be looking at.

### 🎯 Pick With Names

Load a class list once and use any of six pickers.

- **Spinner** — Animated random pick. Toggle "Remove after picked" to avoid repeats, with a tracker showing who's already been called.
- **Single Name** — Instant pick, no animation. Quick and quiet.
- **Lineup** — Randomized order. Good for presentation order.
- **Groups** — Divide the class into groups of 2, 3, 4, 5, or 6.
- **Teams** — Split into exactly 2, 3, 4, 5, or 6 teams.
- **Seating Chart** — Random grid with drag-and-drop to rearrange. Adjustable columns (3–8).

Five spinner styles: **🎡 Wheel** (tick sounds and smooth deceleration), **🎰 Slots** (reel scrolls names vertically), **🃏 Cards** (shuffle face-down, one revealed), **🎱 Lottery** (names bounce as balls, one drops), **💡 Spotlight** (grid with a sweeping highlight).

### 🙋 Pick Without Names

**No roster required.** Instead of picking one student from the class, it picks one student in *every* pair or group at once — using something true about them. Draw a card, read it aloud, and whoever it lands on does the move.

> The one who is closest to the door **answers** for the group.

Works for a class you don't know, a sub, an advisory, a retreat group, or adults in a PD session.

**347 cards** across six topics — 👀 Right Now (54), 📍 Where You Sit (47), 📅 Calendar & Clock (46), 🤝 Get to Know You (101), 🎉 Just for Fun (64), 🔴 Regis Jesuit (35) — split two ways by how long they take to settle:

- **⚡ Quick (224)** — settles in one glance. Reach for these when you just need somebody talking about the question on the board.
- **💬 Conversation (123)** — worth a minute. Good for warm-ups and community building.

**Eight moves.** Answers · Reports out · Explains · Reads aloud · Records · Goes first · Goes last · Sits out. *Goes first* and *Goes last* name a direction so a pod of four knows the order without negotiating.

**Groups, Pairs, or Make Groups.** Groups and Pairs change the wording to fit what's in front of you. **🔀 Make Groups** does something different — one instruction to the whole class that sorts everybody into *new* groups, either by lining up (22 cards) or by finding people who match you (22 cards). Nothing in that deck is based on who's sitting nearby, so students end up next to someone they'd never have chosen.

**Your question on the card.** Type it once and it stays on every card you draw, so students see who is talking *and* what about.

**Make it yours.** Tap ★ on any card to star it — starred cards become their own deck. Write your own cards. Save any deck under a name and it comes back next time.

**🇺🇸 English · 🇪🇸 Español · 🇫🇷 Français.** Every card and the whole panel translate, for Spanish and French classes. The cards are present tense, high-frequency vocabulary, and confirmable by looking around the room — which makes them usable as comprehensible input, not just a picker.

### ⏱️ Timer

Countdown presets from 30 seconds to 5 minutes, or type any custom length. Color-coded warnings, a beep for the last five seconds, and a buzz at zero.

## Features

- **Saved class lists** — Save multiple rosters by name. One tap to load.
- **Sound effects** — Ticks during animations, chime on selection, buzz when the timer ends. Mute button for testing days.
- **Fullscreen** — Hides everything but the tool. Switch between the three modes without leaving fullscreen, so the projector never shows the interface.
- **Light and dark** — Light by default; the toggle remembers your choice.
- **Copy to clipboard** — One click for groups, teams, lineup, seating chart, and question cards. Paste into Teams or Canvas.
- **Backup and restore** — Download everything you've saved as a single file, and restore it on any device.

## Privacy

- No accounts, no login, no ads, no cookies, no tracking
- No data leaves the browser — everything runs locally
- Saved class lists, starred cards, your own cards, and saved decks are stored in your browser only
- Nothing is transmitted anywhere, ever

## ⚠️ Back up your saved lists

Because nothing is stored on a server, **anything you save lives in that one browser on that one computer.** Clearing your browsing data, getting a new machine, switching from Chrome to Edge, or using a lab computer means your saved classes, starred cards, custom cards, and saved decks won't be there.

Open **⚙ Settings** in either Pick With Names or Pick Without Names and use **⬇ Download backup**. You'll get a small `.json` file with everything in it — saved classes, stars, your own cards, saved decks, and your language and theme. Keep it in OneDrive.

To bring it back, or to set up a second computer, use **⬆ Restore from file** and pick that same file. Restoring replaces what's currently in that browser, so back up first if there's anything there you want to keep.

Worth doing at the start of a semester once your class lists are in.

## How to use

1. Visit the link above and bookmark it.
2. Choose a mode at the top.
3. **Pick With Names** — open ⚙ Settings, paste your class list, hit **Load Names**, and optionally **Save as Class** for next time. Settings closes itself and the picker takes over the screen.
4. **Pick Without Names** — nothing to set up. Tap the card and go. Open ⚙ Settings if you want a different deck, a different move, pairs instead of groups, or another language.
5. **Timer** — set a length in ⚙ Settings and hit Start.
6. Hit **⛶ Fullscreen** when you're ready to project.
