How do I get my class list from myRJ or Canvas?
Get all your classes from myRJ at once:
1. In myRJ, open any class and go to Roster → Run Roster/Student Reports → Manual Attendance Sheet - By Teacher And Section.
2. Set Section to All, then choose View/Export → Excel.
3. Open the downloaded file. Click the letter A above the first column to select the whole column, then press Ctrl+C.
4. Click in the box below and press Ctrl+V.
5. Your classes appear as a list. Tick the ones you want, rename them if you like (for example 2RW), and press Save. Each one becomes its own class; everyone is never loaded as one big list.
Step-by-step guide in the Canvas PD course
Or copy from Canvas or myRJ instead: open one class's Canvas People page, its Canvas Gradebook, or its myRJ Roster tab. Scroll to the bottom so everyone loads, press Ctrl+A and then Ctrl+C, and paste below. Only student names are kept.
Pasted from a roster? It gets cleaned for you
Paste straight from myRJ or Canvas — headers, blank rows, emails, parents, grades, Last, First (Preferred) '27 and the class year are all handled.
🔒 Names never leave this computer.Why it’s safe
This protects student privacy. Your roster never leaves your computer. There is no account and no upload — the names stay in this browser, on your machine. RJ Ed Tech never receives them, never stores them, and cannot see them. Pasting your class list here does not share student PII with anyone. If you would still rather not use full names, first name plus last initial works just as well.
Would rather not put a roster in at all? Pick Without Names at the top of the page picks a student in every group using question cards — no list required.

Working in
